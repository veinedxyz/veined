## Provider and network fees

Routes can carry fees that are not {{BRAND}}'s:

- **Network fee / gas** on the source chain, paid in the native asset;
- **Bridge or protocol fees** — for example ONEbridge's network fee, read live from the verifier contract;
- **Routing provider fees**, included in the quote steps.

The route card shows the total fee; the review lists each fee line, whether it is included in the output, and the token it is paid in. Prices used to show fees in USD are informational and never change the transaction.

## Minimums and limits

Some routes have minimum amounts or reserve limits (for example a bridge that releases tokens from a reserve). These are read live and shown before review; a quote that would exceed them is not offered.
