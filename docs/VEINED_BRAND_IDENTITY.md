# VEINED V1.20 brand identity

Status: **recommended owner-review candidate; owner approval open; execution closed**.

VEINED is an intelligent, adaptable multichain workspace. The veined octopus story informs coordinated routes and independent movement, without a literal animal mascot. This milestone retains V1.19’s graphite, sage, ivory, warm architectural illumination and six atmospheric image families. The identity is a precise layer within that existing world, not a replacement art direction.

All alternatives remain available in `public/assets/veined/brand/concepts/`; no concept is a recoloring of another. Every wordmark spells **VEINED**, with six original outlined glyphs and no `.xyz`. The source does not depend on a font file. The softened D and open V balance direct stems and chamfered E terminals.

## Recommended candidate

**C — Quiet Current** is recommended for integration and owner review. The solid outer mass survives favicon scale; its shoulder incision carries a route-merging idea without fine nodes, dots or decorative strands. The rising right terminal creates forward movement, while the broad lower curve makes the mark feel composed rather than sharp or speculative.

## Review surfaces and provenance

Open `/assets/veined/brand/review.html` in the local product preview for all three concepts, both backgrounds, monochrome variants, six actual-size previews and downloadable assets. `comparison.png` is a 1600 × 1080 overview; the gallery additionally shows the 256px previews.

Editable construction: `scripts/brand-sources/veined-geometry.mjs`. Editable SVG masters: `scripts/brand-sources/masters/`. Generator: `scripts/v120-brand-assets.mjs`. These are inside the source checkpoint’s existing `scripts/` scope. Output hashes and sanitization records are in `asset-manifest.json`, `VEINED_ASSET_MANIFEST.md` and this run’s `brand/asset-validation.json`.

The marks and VEINED lettering are original path geometry authored for this task. No stock symbol, traced image, raster upscaling, external icon library or image-generation output is embedded. Local sharp renders raster exports; saxes validates the strict inert SVG subset. Supporting social-card copy uses local Arial/system sans-serif, separately from the path-based primary lettering.

The planned `@veinedxyz` handle remains unconfirmed and has no active profile link. Owner approval of the direction, legal clearance, external audit and any public release remain separate decisions.
